TWRP device tree for Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K / 4K Plus / 4K Max (karat)
=======================================================================

The Amazon Fire TV Stick 4K (2nd Gen), Fire TV Stick 4K Plus and Fire TV Stick 4K Max (2nd Gen) are HDMI streaming devices.

All three share the `karat` codename and the MT8696 platform, and all three are supported by this tree.

The 4K Plus is a 2025 rebrand of the 4K (2nd Gen) rather than new hardware, so the two are identical.

| GPU        | IMG GE9215 @ 650 MHz                 | IMG GE9215 @ 850 MHz |
| RAM        | 2 GB LPDDR4                          | 2 GB LPDDR4 |
| Storage    | 8 GB                                 | 16 GB |
| Wi-Fi      | Wi-Fi 6, dual band                   | Wi-Fi 6E, tri band |
| Bluetooth  | 5.2 + BLE                            | 5.2 + BLE |

| Display    | 1920x1080 over HDMI                  | 1920x1080 over HDMI |
| Video      | 4K @ 60fps, Dolby Vision, HDR10+     | 4K @ 60fps, Dolby Vision, HDR10+ |
